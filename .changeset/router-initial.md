Initial release of the VK Mini Apps router on top of TanStack Router: VKUI
views/panels with static-children animations, modals as routes with
sequential close-then-open choreography and masked global modals, popouts
in history state, VK history with bridge sync (`VKWebAppSetLocation` /
`VKWebAppChangeFragment`), auto swipe-back, deep-link inflate, typed
preset builders with an auto-shell, and a parity navigator. Pairs with
`@vkontakte/vk-bridge` or `@vkma.dev/bridge` (structural contract).
